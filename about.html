About Me

Tell Me About Yourself

I am an Information Science student at the University of South Florida concentrating in cybersecurity. Through my coursework, I have developed experience with Windows and Linux environments, networking, firewall configuration, access controls, system hardening, and other cybersecurity concepts. These experiences have strengthened my interest in understanding how systems operate and how they can be protected from security threats. My professional experience has also helped me develop communication, teamwork, responsibility, and the ability to remain calm under pressure. I hope to combine these interpersonal skills with my growing technical knowledge as I transition from college into the cybersecurity field.

Career Goals

After graduating from the University of South Florida, my goal is to begin a career in cybersecurity, ideally in a security analyst or information security role. I am preparing for this transition by completing my Information Science degree with a concentration in cybersecurity while continuing to develop practical skills in networking, Windows and Linux systems, system hardening, access control, and security configuration. I also plan to pursue industry certifications such as CompTIA Security+ and expand my portfolio with cybersecurity projects. I want to gain additional experience with security monitoring, incident response, scripting, and other tools used by cybersecurity professionals. My goal is to graduate with the education, practical experience, and communication skills necessary to begin my cybersecurity career.
